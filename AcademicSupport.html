Three Layers of Support

Every student is a general-education student first. Extra support adds to the core program; it never replaces it.

For Everyone

Strong Teaching for Every Student

Teachers plan with Universal Design for Learning: choice and real-life relevance, visuals and bilingual supports, and more than one way to show learning. They adjust content, pace and products while keeping expectations high.

When Needed

Extra Help or Extra Challenge

Tutoring, coaching and enrichment, on campus and online. Guided by assessment data and teacher observations, support either reteaches with targeted practice or extends and accelerates.

For Some

An Individual Plan

When a child may need special education or accommodations, the school evaluates with your consent and builds an IEP or 504 plan with you, focused on strengths and clear goals.

A Homeroom Teacher Who Knows Your Child

Chantilly Campus

Learning Support and Special Education

Falcon follows applicable laws, including IDEA and Section 504, to identify students who may need special education or accommodations. Our faculty includes a Special Education Teacher.

  1. 1Share a ConcernA parent or a teacher can ask for an evaluation at any time.
  2. 2Evaluate with Your ConsentA qualified team looks at your child’s needs, and your family takes part in every decision.
  3. 3Plan TogetherAn IEP or 504 plan sets out strengths, measurable goals and accommodations, with access to the full curriculum and school life.
  4. 4Support in Every ClassEvery teacher who works with your child knows the plan. Related services, such as speech-language, OT or counseling, are provided as needed and scheduled to protect core lessons.

Supports Students May Use

  • Extra time, a different setting or tasks in smaller chunks on assessments
  • Text-to-speech, speech-to-text and word prediction
  • Visual supports and organization apps
  • Oral, written or multimedia ways to show learning
  • Field trips, clubs and events planned with your family

English and Arabic Language Support

At Falcon, all teachers are language teachers and all students are language learners. Each child’s home language is valued, too.

Students Learning English

  • Daily designated English instruction
  • GLAD strategies that open up content in every subject
  • Small-group push-in support and online tutoring from Literacy Specialists

Arabic at the Right Level

  • Arabic five times a week in KG – G5
  • From Grade 6, a placement test sets the level: Beginner, Intermediate or Advanced
  • Bilingual glossaries, sentence frames and heritage-learner enrichment

Families in Their Language

  • All written communication translated into Arabic
  • Free simultaneous Arabic interpretation at community events
  • Translators for conferences and meetings when needed

Clear Feedback All Year

Students always know what they are learning, why they are learning it and how they will know they have learned it.

Feedback in Every Unit

Quick checks, rubrics and conferences during each unit guide the next step before the final task.

KG – G5 Report Cards

Standards-based report cards twice a year, with comments from English and Arabic teachers, plus MAP testing to track growth.

G6 – G8 IB Criteria

Work is scored against IB MYP criteria, with a final 1–7 score each semester, in January and June.

Three Kinds of Conferences

Fall parent-teacher conferences, student-led conferences and teacher-student goal-setting conferences.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I ask for an evaluation for my child?
Talk to your child’s teacher or the front office. Families or staff can ask for an evaluation at any time, and it only takes place with your consent.
Will my child be taken out of class for support?
Support is planned to protect core lessons. Much of it happens inside the classroom: specialists support English and math through a push-in model, and related services are scheduled around core instruction.
My child is still learning English. What help is there?
Daily designated English instruction, GLAD strategies in every subject, and small-group push-in support and online tutoring from Literacy Specialists for students who need it.
Is there support for students who need more challenge?
Yes. Academic support can level up as well as scaffold, with extension and acceleration based on assessment data. In high school, students can add Honors, AP courses and NOVA college courses.
How does Falcon teach honest work?
Teachers teach citation, quoting and paraphrasing, and students credit every source, including AI tools. In KG – G8, a first incident usually means redoing the work and a call home, and repeated incidents go to the principal.
