Ashburn Campus Questions

When will the Ashburn campus open?
The Ashburn campus is scheduled to open in fall 2026.
Which grades will it serve?
Grades 6 through 12. For 2026–27, enrollment opens February 1 for Grades 6–11. Enrollment for Grade 12 launches in 2027–28.
Where is it, and how easy is it to reach?
20577 Ashburn Road, Ashburn, VA 20147, close to Route 7 and Route 267 and easy to reach from Loudoun and Fairfax counties.
Is transportation offered?
Yes. Falcon provides transportation to families in more than 10 cities and regions across Northern Virginia, including Falls Church, Sterling, Fairfax County, Loudoun County and Centreville, and is expanding to more communities.
How many students will it hold?
The projected capacity is 150 students.
What academic approach will it follow?
We follow the IB approach, aligned with Cognia, with STEAM projects where students use what they learn in class. Dual enrollment is planned for high school students.
Will there be campus tours and a town hall?
Yes. Tours of the Ashburn campus are announced on our website and shared with families who applied or joined the interest list. You can also register there for our next town hall meeting.

See the Ashburn Campus in Person

Tours run Sundays, 2–4 PM, and take about 45 minutes.
