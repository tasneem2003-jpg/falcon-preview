Elementary School Fire Department Visit

Meet the People Who Help Keep Our Community Safe.

Our elementary students will welcome a special visit from the fire department at Falcon Academy’s Chantilly Campus.

This visit offers an opportunity to learn about firefighters’ work, explore the importance of fire safety, and appreciate the responsibility and care involved in serving our community.

A Chance to Learn About

  • Community Helpers: The role firefighters play in protecting others.
  • Fire Safety: Why awareness and safe choices matter.
  • Service and Responsibility: How caring for others makes a difference.
