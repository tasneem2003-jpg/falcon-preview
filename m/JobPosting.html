About the role

Arabic is taught in every grade at Falcon, from kindergarten through high school. As an Arabic Teacher, you'll teach [Grades] at our [Campus] campus, in classes that bring together children who speak Arabic at home and children who are just starting out. You'll help shape how a new IB Candidate School teaches Arabic, with colleagues who see the language as part of who our students are.

Responsibilities

  • Plan and teach Arabic lessons aligned with IB language acquisition.
  • Differentiate for native speakers, heritage learners and students new to Arabic.
  • Assess progress and report clearly to families.
  • Contribute to Arabic Language Day, poetry and Quran events.
  • Collaborate with homeroom and Islamic Studies teachers.
  • Take part in IB training and professional development.

Qualifications

Required

  • Fluency in Modern Standard Arabic, spoken and written
  • Bachelor's degree [field to confirm]
  • [x] years of experience teaching Arabic to children or teens

Preferred

  • Experience with the IB Primary Years or Middle Years Programme
  • Experience teaching heritage learners and students new to Arabic
  • A teaching license (Virginia or another state)
  • Experience preparing students for poetry or Quran events

About Falcon Academy

Falcon was founded in 2025 by parents and alumni of King Abdullah Academy to carry its 40-year community forward. We serve kindergarten through Grade 12 on two campuses in Ashburn and Chantilly, Virginia, with a 10:1 student-to-teacher ratio. Every teacher and staff member who joined in our first year became a co-owner of the school.

Benefits

Full-time staff receive health coverage, a retirement plan, professional development and IB training, a tuition discount for staff children, paid time off, and Islamic holidays observed. [Confirm with HR]
