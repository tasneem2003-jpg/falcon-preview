What Falcon Offers International Families

An internationally minded school with Islamic values, where your child can keep their languages and prepare for university.

Arabic and English, Side by Side

Bilingual Arabic and English teachers and support staff. Arabic is taught every day by specialist teachers, grouped by level and age for native, heritage and new learners.

Help Settling In

A new school in a new country is a big step. Here is how we support your child and your family.

  • English Language Support

    Students still learning English get daily designated English instruction, GLAD strategies, push-in support and online tutoring.

  • Communication in Arabic

    All written communication to families is translated into Arabic, with free interpretation at community events and translators for meetings when needed.

  • Home Languages Valued

    We encourage students to keep developing their home language. Our libraries hold resources in Arabic.

  • Small Classes

    A 10:1 student–teacher ratio, so every teacher knows each child’s progress.

  • Getting to School

    Transportation reaches families in more than 10 cities and regions, from $2,500 a year, and Extended Care runs until 5:30 PM.

  • Halal School Lunch

    Healthy, halal meals at $8 a meal, ordered in advance and billed monthly.

Questions from International Families

More answers are in our Admissions FAQ.

Do we need to live in a particular school district?

No. Falcon admits students on a space-available basis, and there is no district residency requirement.

Admissions Policy→

Can my child join without speaking Arabic?

Yes. Arabic classes are for native, heritage and new learners, grouped by level and age. In G6 – G10, a placement test places each student in a beginner, intermediate or advanced level.

Arabic Language→

Is there support if my child is still learning English?

Yes. English language learners get daily designated English instruction, GLAD strategies, push-in support and online tutoring.

Language Policy→

Can we see the school before we move?

A 90-second video tour, filmed at the Chantilly campus, is on our Visit page. Once you arrive, tours run Sundays at Ashburn and Saturdays at Chantilly, 2–4 PM.
