Falcon Academy Is Now an IB World School for the Middle Years Programme

One community. A shared vision. An achievement to celebrate.

We are delighted to announce that Falcon Academy is officially authorized as an International Baccalaureate (IB) World School for the Middle Years Programme (MYP).

Achieved so soon after opening our doors, this milestone reflects the dedication, preparation, and teamwork of the people who bring Falcon’s vision to life every day.

To Our Leadership, Teachers, and Staff

Your commitment to our students, professional growth, and thoughtful teaching has made this achievement possible. Thank you for the care and effort behind every step of this journey.

To Our Falcon Families

Thank you for trusting us with your children’s education and supporting our growing school community. Your partnership is an essential part of this success.

To Our Students

Your curiosity, effort, and willingness to learn give this milestone its meaning. You remain at the heart of everything we do.

As we join the global community of IB World Schools, we look forward to building on this foundation: connecting academic learning, critical thinking, and global understanding with the Islamic values of integrity, respect, and responsibility.

Congratulations, Team Falcon. This achievement belongs to our whole community!
