Our Campuses

Falcon has two campuses, and your child’s grade decides which one. Middle and high school are in Ashburn, kindergarten to G5 in Chantilly.

New Fall 2026

G6 – G12Middle & High School

Ashburn Campus

A building of their own for our middle and high school students, with labs for STEAM and room for AP and college-credit courses.

  • IB Middle Years Programme (authorized)
  • AP courses and dual enrollment with NOVA
  • Award-winning building on a 3.04-acre site
