What AP is

Advanced Placement courses follow a college-level curriculum set by the College Board. Students take them in high school, then sit a national AP exam scored from 1 to 5.

College-level work, at Falcon

Students take AP classes on our Ashburn campus, with Falcon teachers and the support of their high school.

Credit and placement

Many universities give credit or let students skip intro courses for scores of 3 or higher. Each college sets its own rules.

Stronger applications

AP courses show colleges that a student chose a challenging schedule and did well in it.

How it works

From choosing a course to sending scores to colleges.

  1. 1Choose courses with a counselorLook at grades, interests and the intended major, and keep the workload manageable.
  2. 2Take the course at FalconTaught by a Falcon teacher, following the College Board’s AP course framework.
  3. 3Sit the AP exam in MayEach exam is scored from 1 to 5. Falcon is an approved College Board testing school.
  4. 4Send scores to collegesStudents choose which colleges receive their scores. Each college decides what credit it gives.

College Board Level I and Level II approval

Falcon Academy earned College Board Level I and Level II approval in July 2026. This means Falcon can:

Offer AP coursesAnd take part in the College Board’s AP Program.
Give College Board examsAs an approved testing school for eligible assessments.
Request accommodationsSubmit testing accommodations through College Board SSD.
Help with SAT fee waiversApply for waivers for eligible students.

Falcon also receives AP, PSAT and SAT score reports, so counselors can follow each student’s progress.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can take AP courses?
High school students who are ready for college-level work, with a counselor’s advice. [Grade levels and prerequisites for each course.]
Where do students take the AP exams?
Falcon is an approved College Board testing school, so eligible exams can be given at Falcon. [Confirm exam location and dates for 2026–27.]
Do AP scores count for college credit?
Often, yes. Many colleges give credit or advanced placement for scores of 3 or higher, but each college sets its own policy. Check with the colleges your child is considering.
Is there a fee for AP exams?
The College Board charges a fee for each exam. [Exam fee and whether Falcon covers any part of it.]
Can my child get testing accommodations?
If your child is eligible, Falcon can request accommodations through the College Board’s Services for Students with Disabilities (SSD).
Should my child choose AP or dual enrollment?
Many students do both. AP courses are taught at Falcon and end with a national exam. Dual enrollment courses are NOVA classes that go on a college transcript. A counselor helps plan the right mix.
