Our Parent-Teacher-Student Organization brings families, teachers and students together to support learning, celebrate milestones and grow a close-knit school community.

Get Involved ↓ Contact the PTSO

What Is the PTSO?

The PTSO is Falcon’s parent-teacher-student organization. It gives families an easy way to take part in school life, work side by side with teachers, and help students feel supported in everything they do.

Every family is part of the Falcon community. The PTSO is where that community comes together.

  • Connect Families

    Welcome new families and help everyone feel at home, from KG to G12.

  • Support Teachers and Students

    Give time, ideas and helping hands that make classroom life richer.

  • Celebrate Together

    Help plan and host the events and milestones that bring Falcon families together.

Why Families Join

A small amount of time goes a long way for your child and the school.

Know Your School

Meet teachers, staff and other families, and see school life up close.

Support Your Child

Children feel proud when they see their family involved at school.

Share Your Ideas

Bring suggestions and help shape events and activities for students.

Build Friendships

Get to know families who share your values and your hopes for your children.

Ways to Get Involved

Give what fits your schedule, whether that is one afternoon or a whole season.

Volunteer at Events

Lend a hand at family events, townhalls, celebrations and ceremonies through the year.

Support Teachers

Help with classroom projects, teacher appreciation and the small things that make lessons richer.

Cheer On Students

Come out for competitions, award days and performances, and help make each one memorable.

Serve Together

Join service projects with students, teachers and families, from neighborhood clean-ups to causes close to home.
