EVERY GRADE · BOTH CAMPUSES

Arabic & Islamic Studies

Arabic language, Quran and Islamic Studies are part of every school day, from Kindergarten to Grade 12. Specialist teachers help each child grow in faith, in values and in the language of the Quran.

  • Arabic language every day, taught by specialist teachers
  • Quran recitation and memorization
  • Islamic Studies that is age-appropriate and personally meaningful
  • The Annual Quran Competition and the Arabic Poetry Competition
