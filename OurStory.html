Our Story

When King Abdullah Academy closed its doors, parents and students realized we were not only losing a building — we were at risk of losing our home: the community. Falcon Academy was born out of love for that home.

On this page History Mission & Vision Values Founding families

History

From King Abdullah Academy to Falcon

Falcon is a new school with a long story behind it. These are the moments that brought us here.

  1. 1984

    Our roots

    Our community begins at the Islamic Saudi Academy in Northern Virginia, later renamed King Abdullah Academy. Over four decades it becomes the school at the heart of this community.

    [Confirm founding year with the school]

    [KAA archive photo]
  2. 2025

    A community decides to stay together

    King Abdullah Academy closes. Its parents and alumni refuse to let the community scatter, and together they found Falcon Academy: a new school built to keep families together and to raise the academic bar for the next generation.

  3. FALL2025

    First day in Chantilly

    Falcon opens its doors at 42851 Smallwood Terrace in Chantilly and becomes an IB Candidate School for the Primary Years and Middle Years Programmes.

  4. JUN2026

    Our first graduation

    The Class of 2026 becomes Falcon's first graduating class, celebrated by the families and teachers who built the school together in its first year.

    See graduation photos ›
  5. JUL2026

    College Board Level I and II approval

    On Jul 20, 2026, Falcon earns College Board Level I and Level II approval. A few weeks earlier, on Jun 26, 2026, we announced a dual enrollment partnership with Northern Virginia Community College (NOVA).

  6. FALL2026
    Where we are today

    Ashburn campus opens

    Middle and high school students move into our second campus at 20577 Ashburn Road. Dual enrollment with NOVA begins, so high school students can take college courses, and in September Cognia visits Falcon as part of our accreditation review.

Mission & vision

Our mission

Falcon Academy provides a caring, challenging, inclusive, and safe learning environment that empowers students to achieve academic excellence, develop proficiency in the Arabic language, and live by Islamic values.

As an IB Candidate School founded by the King Abdullah Academy community, we hold every child to high standards in a school that feels like home. These principles shape daily learning at Falcon:

  • Academic excellence for every child, through IB inquiry and small classes with one teacher for every ten students.
  • Proficiency in the Arabic language, from Kindergarten through Grade 12.
  • Islamic values lived every day, through prayer, Quran and service to others.
  • A caring, challenging, inclusive and safe place to learn, where every child is known by name.

Our vision

To be a nurturing learning community that develops well-rounded, academically accomplished, and globally aware leaders who are grounded in their Islamic identity, connected to their Arabic language and heritage, and prepared to contribute with compassion, integrity, and respect in a diverse world.

Falcon is an IB Candidate School and a candidate for Cognia accreditation. Accreditation and IB candidacy ›

Our values

Six values, one word: Falcon

Our values are woven into every grade and both campuses. Each one shows up in an ordinary school day, not only on a poster.

  • Faith

    Daily prayer together, and Quran and Islamic Studies in every grade.

  • Academic excellence

    IB inquiry units and small classes, with one teacher for every ten students.

  • Leadership

    Student-led projects, and Service as Action in the Middle Years, where students plan and carry out real service.

  • Community

    Family events, townhalls and service projects with classmates, teachers and families.

  • Ownership

    Setting learning goals, reflecting on progress, and the MYP Personal Project, chosen and led by the student.

  • Nurture

    A homeroom teacher who knows your child well, with counseling and special education support when extra help is needed.
