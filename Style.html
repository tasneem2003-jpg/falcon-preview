Pattern A · marketing pages

Home, Academics, Admissions. Sub-tabs under the header, a navy hero (or a white hero with three fact cards), sections alternating white and light grey, card grids, one FAQ, and a navy closing band with Book a Tour + Apply.

Pattern B · utility pages

Community, About, Careers, Contact. Grey ground, breadcrumb, optional pills, then the whole page in one rounded panel: title, filter chips, label-and-rule groups, rows with one clear action each.
