Arabic & Islamic Studies

Arabic and Islamic Studies sit at the heart of Falcon’s mission: to help every student achieve academic excellence, develop proficiency in the Arabic language, and live by Islamic values.

Arabic Language Program

A journey from elementary through middle school. Students learn to communicate with confidence, appreciate Arabic literature and culture, and understand the language in both academic and real-life settings.

Students are grouped by both age and Arabic level (beginner, capable or efficient), so every child is supported and challenged at the right level. Native, heritage and second-language learners each get the support they need.

Elementary school

Building the foundations

Listening, speaking, reading and writing, learned through real, joyful experiences.

  • Stories, songs, conversations, pictures and games
  • Inquiry units built on form, function, meaning and audience
  • Leveled readers, visual aids, sentence starters and peer modeling
Middle school

Reading deeper, writing with purpose

More complex language, literature and culture, and confident spoken and written Arabic.

  • Short stories, poems, articles, speeches, ads and news reports
  • Analyzing themes, tone, perspective and style
  • Creative and analytical writing, presentations and multimedia projects
  • Grammar, vocabulary and accurate use of the language

Along the way

Conversations, dictations, reflective journals, reading logs and peer feedback show how each student is growing.

To show what they’ve learned

Interviews, story retellings, debates, blog writing, cultural presentations and text analysis.

Islamic Studies

A cornerstone of our mission. Islamic Studies nurtures each student’s faith, values and understanding of Islam in ways that are deeply rooted, age-appropriate and personally meaningful, building spiritual awareness, ethical reasoning and a strong Islamic identity.

Elementary school

Learning faith by living it

Stories from the Qur’an and Sunnah, hands-on activities, role-play and daily reflection.

‘AqeedahIbadahAkhlaqSeerahIslamic history
  • Memorizing and reciting short surahs, with understanding
  • Prayer routines, respectful dialogue and working together
  • Support for every child, whether new to these ideas or not
Middle school

Thinking deeply about faith

Reflection, inquiry and applying Islamic teachings to today’s world, in Arabic and English.

TawheedFiqhEthicsProphets & companionsMuslim history
  • Reading the Qur’an and Hadith closely, and tafsir sessions
  • Debates, service learning and multimedia projects
  • Student-led khutbahs and presentations

Along the way

Reflection journals, class discussions, recitation checks and self-assessment.

To show what they’ve learned

Projects such as planning a zakat campaign, writing a khutbah, case studies or a digital Seerah timeline.

Our goal: compassionate, responsible and spiritually grounded young people who are proud of their faith and ready to serve their communities.
