Arabic & Islamic Studies

Arabic language, Quran and Islamic Studies are part of every school day, from Kindergarten to Grade 12. Children grow in faith, in values and in a language that connects them to the Quran and to their heritage.

Our mission is to empower every student to “develop proficiency in the Arabic language, and live by Islamic values.”

Faith that grows with your child

Our Islamic Studies program aims to nurture each student's faith and values, and to build an understanding of Islam that is age-appropriate and personally meaningful.

That means a five-year-old and a fifteen-year-old learn about the same faith in very different ways. Every lesson balances three things: concepts, skills and content.

Concepts

The big ideas of belief, worship and character, revisited and deepened as children grow.

Skills

Reciting, reading, reflecting, asking good questions and applying what they learn in daily life.

Content

The Quran, the life of the Prophet, the stories of the prophets and Islamic history.
