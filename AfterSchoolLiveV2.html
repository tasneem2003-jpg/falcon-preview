Questions families ask

Clubs & days

How do the clubs work?

On weekdays your child joins one club a day after school, on the days you choose. Weekend clubs run on Saturdays and Sundays. You pay per club day: $35 on weekdays and $50 on weekends, weekly or monthly, as you prefer.

Where do the clubs take place?

Weekday clubs for KG–Grade 5 are at the Chantilly campus, and for Grades 6–11 at the Ashburn campus. Weekend clubs are at Ashburn, and Music & School Band is at Bera Music School.

Is there pickup from my child’s school?

Yes. A shuttle can pick students up from school. Ask the front office about your school and route. [Shuttle price and schools served]

Can my child come only on some days?

Yes. You choose the days, and you pay only for those days.

Do I pay weekly or monthly?

Either. You decide whether to pay for one week at a time or for the month.

Clubs & teachers

Do the language clubs need prior experience?

No. Turkish, French and Spanish are interactive beginner clubs, using games, role-play, songs and stories.

Who can join Self-Defense & Martial Arts?

It is for girls only. Students learn age-appropriate self-defense with a focus on safety, respect and self-control.

How do the STEM and robotics clubs work?

They are hands-on. Solar and Wind Energy prepares students for the KidWind Challenge, and NASA / Aerospace includes design challenges and experiments. On weekends, iCode instructors lead VEX Robotics and Humanoid AI Programming, and MathCON prepares middle schoolers for the MathCON competition.

Who teaches the clubs?

Falcon teachers and specialist partners, including Alia Raviola, Mehmet Sonmez, Emine Kilic, Rabia Nur Demir, Rida Ahsan and Sule Cetil. Weekend robotics and AI clubs are led by iCode instructors, and Music by Bera Music School.

Registration

How do I register?

Register online on the Edumagined Parent Portal, the same way as Summer Camp. For Music & School Band, register through Bera Music School’s form.
