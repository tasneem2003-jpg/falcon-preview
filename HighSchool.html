Your Path to College

One plan covers all four years, and families take part at every step.

  1. 1

    Grade 9

    Settle into high school and build a four-year course plan with the counselor. Students ready for more can start Honors and NOVA college courses. PSAT 8/9 at school.

  2. 2

    Grade 10

    Strengthen study habits and grades. Ready students take their first AP course. PSAT at school.

  3. 3

    Grade 11

    More AP and NOVA college courses. PSAT/NMSQT and SAT, and start building a college list.

  4. 4

    Grade 12

    College applications, essays and recommendation letters with counselor support. Final AP exams and graduation.

Families plan with us. On Jul 20, 2026, Falcon families joined us for an evening on course planning and getting ready for college.
