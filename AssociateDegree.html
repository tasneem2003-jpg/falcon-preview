Associate Degree Pathway

With careful planning, Falcon high school students can take NOVA college courses from Grade 9 and work toward some or all of an associate degree before they finish high school.

What the pathway is

An associate degree is a two-year college degree. Falcon students work toward one by taking NOVA courses through dual enrollment while they stay at Falcon. Every NOVA course they pass adds college credit on a NOVA transcript.

The associate degree is an optional goal, not a guarantee. It depends on course availability, eligibility, grades and time.

FWhat Falcon does

  • Builds a plan with your family from Grade 9
  • Fits NOVA courses around the high school schedule
  • Confirms which NOVA courses also count for the Falcon diploma
  • Reviews progress with each student and family

NWhat NOVA does

  • Admission, placement and registration
  • Teaching, grading and the college transcript
  • Degree requirements and advising
  • Tuition and fees

Why it helps

Students start college early, with their high school behind them.

Start university ahead

Credits that transfer can let students skip intro courses or enter university further along.

Save time and tuition

Fewer courses to take at university can mean less time and lower cost later.

Every course counts

Even without the full degree, each NOVA course passed stays on the student’s college transcript.

A plan built around a major

Counselors match NOVA courses to the subject your child wants to study.

Real college experience

College classes, deadlines and independence, practiced with high school support.

Stronger applications

College grades show universities that a student can handle college-level work.

The pathway, step by step

The earlier a student plans, the more college credit fits into high school.

  1. 1Plan in Grade 9 FalconMeet a counselor to talk about goals, the intended major and how much college work fits.
  2. 2Get ready for NOVA NOVAApply to NOVA and show English and math readiness with grades, test scores or AP results.
  3. 3Take NOVA courses each term NOVAAdd college courses alongside Falcon classes, often after Honors or AP courses in the same subject.
  4. 4Review the plan every year FalconThe counselor and a NOVA advisor check credits against the degree requirements.
  5. 5Graduate with college creditStudents finish with a Falcon diploma and NOVA credits. Those who complete every requirement also earn the associate degree.

Is it right for my child?

The pathway suits students who:

  • Plan to go to universityAnd have an idea of what they want to study.
  • Are ready for college-level workStrong reading, writing and math, shown in school records.
  • Manage their time wellCollege deadlines run alongside high school work.
  • Want to start earlyThe pathway works best when it begins in Grade 9 or 10.

Credit and cost

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the associate degree guaranteed?
No. It is an optional goal. Whether a student finishes depends on course availability, eligibility, grades and time. Every course they pass still counts as college credit.
How is this different from dual enrollment?
Dual enrollment means taking NOVA courses while at Falcon. The associate degree pathway is a plan to take enough of the right NOVA courses to complete a degree.
Can my child still take AP courses?
Yes. Many students combine AP courses at Falcon with NOVA courses, and AP exam results can help show readiness for NOVA.
Which associate degree can my child earn?
[Which NOVA associate degrees fit the pathway.] A counselor and a NOVA advisor choose the degree that matches the student’s intended major.
Will NOVA courses count toward the Falcon diploma?
Only if Falcon approves it. Your child should confirm with a counselor before registering for each course.
Who pays for NOVA courses?
Families pay NOVA’s tuition and fees directly. [Whether Falcon contributes.]
