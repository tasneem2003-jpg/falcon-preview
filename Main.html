IB and Accreditation

Now an IB World School for the MYP

Falcon Academy is an IB World School for the Middle Years Programme (MYP), authorized in November 2026, and a Candidate School* for the Primary Years Programme (PYP). The school is pursuing IB authorization for the Primary Years Programme. IB World Schools share a common philosophy—a commitment to high quality, challenging, international education that Falcon Academy believes is important for our students.

Falcon Academy is also a Candidate for Accreditation with NCA CASI, NWAC and SACS CASI.

Accreditation Details →
Advanced Placement

College Board Approved AP Courses

17 College Board AP courses and 22 Honors courses, approved at Level I and II. Each AP course ends with the AP exam in May. Explore AP Courses → Associate degree pathway

College Credit from Grade 9

Four NOVA Associate of Science tracks (Engineering, IT, Medical, Business), with up to 60 college credits. Explore the Pathway →

*Only schools authorized by the IB Organization can offer any of its four academic programmes: the Primary Years Programme (PYP), the Middle Years Programme (MYP), the Diploma Programme, or the Career-related Programme (CP). Candidate status gives no guarantee that authorization will be granted. For further information about the IB and its programmes, visit www.ibo.org. The associate degree is an optional goal and is not guaranteed.
