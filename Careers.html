Careers at Falcon Academy

Help build a school with a 40‑year community behind it.

Falcon Academy is an IB Candidate School in Northern Virginia, founded in 2025 by the parents and alumni of King Abdullah Academy to carry their community forward. We teach Kindergarten through Grade 12 on two campuses, Chantilly and Ashburn, in small classes with one teacher for every ten students. Every teacher and staff member who joined in our first year became a co-owner of the school, and our teachers are trained in the IB to teach through inquiry, with Arabic and Islamic values part of every day. If you want your work to shape how a school grows, we would like to hear from you.

Why teach at Falcon

You won't be joining a finished school. You'll help build one, with colleagues who care about the same things you do.

Co-ownership

Every teacher and staff member who joined in our first year is a co-owner of the school. Your ideas shape how Falcon grows.

Small classes, 10:1

One teacher for every ten students, so you have time to know each child and teach them well.

IB training

Falcon is an IB Candidate School for the Primary Years and Middle Years Programmes, and our teachers are trained in IB teaching.

An Islamic environment

Prayer, Quran and Islamic values are part of the school day, alongside Arabic in every grade.
