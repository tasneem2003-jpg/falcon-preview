Applying

Who can apply, what you need and what happens next.

Which grades are you enrolling for 2026–27?

Kindergarten to Grade 12. Grades 6 to 12 enroll at the Ashburn campus, and Kindergarten to Grade 5 at the Chantilly campus.

How to Apply→

How do I apply?

In three steps, online: start one application for all the children you are enrolling, pay the $150 application fee for each child, then complete your assigned tasks and upload the required documents in your parent portal.

How to Apply→

Can I apply for more than one child at once?

Yes. One online application covers all your children, so you enter your family details once. The $150 fee is charged per child, inside the same form.

How to Apply→

What is the application fee, and is it refundable?

It is $150 per child, paid by credit card with the application form. It is non-refundable, covers processing and the placement assessment, and is the only fee before enrollment.

Tuition & Financial Aid→

Which documents will I need?

Your child’s birth certificate, last report card and immunization record. You upload them online in your parent portal.

How to Apply→

What happens after I apply?

We contact you to schedule a short placement visit. Applications are reviewed as space allows, and once every checklist item is in, including standardized test results and any placement test or interview for new students, you receive the decision letter and contract electronically.

How to Apply→

Do you accept transfers from public schools?

Yes, transfers from public schools are welcome. We review the last report card and run a short placement assessment.

Admissions Overview→

Who can apply, and do we need to live in a certain district?

Falcon admits all students on a space-available basis and does not discriminate on the basis of disability, gender, nationality, race or ethnicity, religion, sexual orientation or other protected characteristics. There is no district residency requirement.

Admissions Policy→

Do you welcome students of all faiths?

Yes. Falcon is faith-based and integrates Islamic principles into its educational philosophy, while welcoming and respecting students of all faiths.

Admissions Policy→

Do you support students with special education needs?

From the school’s second year, Falcon is open to students with special education needs and supports Special Education services.

Admissions Policy→

Tuition and Fees

What you pay, how you pay and how to save.

How much is tuition for 2026–27?

Tuition is $13,350 per year, the same for every grade, or $1,335 a month over 10 months. Books and technology are $600 per student per year.

Tuition & Financial Aid→

Which payment plans can I choose?

10 monthly payments of $1,335 (Aug 15, 2026 to May 15, 2027), 2 payments of $6,675 (Aug 15, 2026 and Jan 15, 2027), or pay in full by Aug 1, 2026 and get 5% off tuition. You choose your plan when you sign the enrollment contract.

Payment Plans→

Is there a sibling discount?

Yes. Tuition is $450 lower for every child after the first, applied automatically.

Tuition & Financial Aid→

Do you offer financial aid?

Yes, through the Falcon Family Support Program. Aid is need-based and confidential, reviewed through SSS (school code 201332), and the deadline is July 1. It covers base tuition, and applying never affects admission.

Financial Aid→

Are there other ways to save?

A $500 referral credit for both families when a Falcon family refers you, and a $1,000 Founding Family Voucher if you have a Legacy Voucher code (one code per family, valid Feb 1 – Aug 1). Both apply automatically once the name or code is in your application.

Ways to Save→

Which services cost extra?

All are optional: transportation from $2,500 a year on selected routes, school lunch at $8 a meal, Extended Care at $35 a day, and the yearbook, billed in spring.

Fees and Services→

Visiting

Tours, what to expect and other ways to see Falcon.

When can we tour a campus?

Tours run Sundays at Ashburn and Saturdays at Chantilly, 2–4 PM. They are free, take about 45 minutes and come with no commitment.

Visit & Book a Tour→

What happens on a tour?

You are welcomed at the front desk, with free parking on site. You see classrooms and the playground with a real class in session, then meet the admissions director to ask about tuition, aid, transport or anything else. Bring your child.

Visit & Book a Tour→

What if none of the tour dates work for us?

Call 571-800-1382 and we will suggest a time.

Do we have to tour before applying?

No. You can apply first and tour later. Many families do.

How to Apply→

Can we see the school online first?

Yes. A 90-second video tour, filmed at the Chantilly campus, is on our Visit page.

Visit & Book a Tour→

Campuses and Transport

Where your child learns and how they get there.

Which campus will my child attend?

Your child’s grade decides. Middle and high school (G6 – G12) are at the Ashburn campus, and Kindergarten to Grade 5 at the Chantilly campus.

Our Campuses→

Where are the campuses?

The Ashburn campus is at 20577 Ashburn Road, Ashburn, VA 20147, close to Route 7 and Route 267. The Chantilly campus is at 42851 Smallwood Terrace, Chantilly, VA 20152. Both are easy to reach from Loudoun and Fairfax counties.

Ashburn Campus→Chantilly Campus→

What are the school hours?

The school day runs 8:00am – 3:00pm at both campuses, with Extended Care until 5:30 PM.

Our Campuses→

Is transportation offered?

Yes. Transportation reaches families in more than 10 cities and regions, including Falls Church, Sterling, Fairfax County, Loudoun County and Centreville. It starts at $2,500 a year on selected routes and is billed with tuition.

Fees and Services→

Is there a shuttle between the campuses?

Yes. A complimentary shuttle runs between our campuses for siblings at both campuses and for families who live near the other campus.

Our Campuses→

What is the Ashburn campus like?

A modern, award-winning building of their own for our middle and high school students, opened in fall 2026: 15,148 square feet on a 3.04-acre site, with labs for STEAM and a projected capacity of 150 students.

Ashburn Campus→

How do you keep young children safe at Chantilly?

The Chantilly building is made for young children, with locked doors, a fenced play area and on-site security.

Chantilly Campus→

Academics and Credentials

Accreditation, curriculum and college-level options.

Is Falcon accredited?

Falcon Academy is an IB World School for the Middle Years Programme (MYP), an IB Candidate School for the Primary Years Programme (PYP), and a Candidate for Accreditation with Cognia.

Accreditation→

What curriculum do you follow?

An inquiry-based curriculum following the IB frameworks: the Middle Years Programme in G6 – G10, and the Primary Years Programme in KG – G5, where Falcon is an IB Candidate School.

Accreditation→

Do you offer AP courses?

Yes. Eight AP courses in math, science, history, government and economics, taught at Falcon by our own teachers. Falcon earned College Board Level I and Level II approval in July 2026 and is an approved testing school, with AP exams every May.

AP Courses→

Can high school students earn college credit?

Yes, through dual enrollment with Northern Virginia Community College (NOVA). Students can take NOVA courses from Grade 9 if they meet NOVA’s requirements, and families pay NOVA’s tuition and fees directly. A NOVA course counts toward the Falcon diploma only if Falcon approves it.

Dual Enrollment→

Can my child earn an associate degree?

Some students may, with careful planning with Falcon counselors and NOVA advisors. It depends on course availability, eligibility, grades and time, and it is not guaranteed.

Associate Degree→

Are Arabic and Islamic Studies part of the program?

Yes. Both are taught in every grade, at both campuses. Arabic is taught every day by specialist teachers, with students grouped by level and age, so native, heritage and new learners all work at the right level.

Arabic Language→

How big are classes, and who teaches them?

Falcon has a 10:1 student–teacher ratio. 95% of our teachers are IB-trained, and over 55% hold a master’s degree.

Accreditation→

How do you track my child’s progress?

With MAP (Measures of Academic Progress) testing three times a year. It tracks each child’s growth against national benchmarks, so teachers can adjust instruction.

Accreditation→

Daily Life

Care, meals and support around the school day.

Do you offer after-school care?

Yes. Extended Care runs 3:00 – 5:30 PM every school day for all grades, KG – G12, at $35 a day, billed monthly. It is closed on holidays, snow days and early-dismissal days.

Extended Care→

What do children do in Extended Care?

Supervised care with homework help, clubs and free play. Activities are optional, so children can read, study or do homework instead.

Extended Care→

Is school lunch available?

Yes. Healthy, halal meals cost $8 a meal, ordered in advance and billed monthly.

Fees and Services→

Is there support for students still learning English?

Yes. English language learners get daily designated English instruction, GLAD strategies, push-in support and online tutoring.

Language Policy→
