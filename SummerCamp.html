Seven weeks, seven themes

Each week has its own theme, and every Friday ends with a field trip or a celebration. Come for one week or all seven.

  1. Week 1Jun 29 – Jul 3

    Outdoor Adventures

    Backyard biology and nature-based engineering challenges, exploring the science and language of the world around us.

    Friday: Field trip to Claude Moore Park

  2. Week 2Jul 6 – 10

    Kitchen Chemistry

    Follow recipes in Spanish and Arabic, measure ingredients and explore the chemistry that makes food taste great.

    Friday: Field trip to a local restaurant

  3. Week 3Jul 13 – 17

    Wonders of Water

    Oceans, lakes and weather: water’s properties, why it matters to our planet and the life it supports.

    Friday: Hal & Berni Hanson Park and splash pad

  4. Week 4Jul 20 – 24

    Music & Movement

    Rhythm and sound, songs in new languages, and how music connects cultures around the world.

    Friday: Student performance and party with families

  5. Week 5Jul 27 – 31

    Spectacular Space

    Build model rockets, learn the constellations and investigate weather patterns here on Earth.

    Friday: Field trip to the Udvar-Hazy Center

  6. Week 6Aug 3 – 7

    Global Games

    Sports and games from around the world, from a mini-Olympics to the World Cup: teamwork and friendly competition.

    Friday: Field Day

  7. Week 7Aug 10 – 14

    Be a Hero!

    Hands-on service projects and team challenges that build character and show campers they can make a difference.

    Friday: Service project presentations and party

More questions

Prices, refunds and safety are answered above. Here is everything else.

Languages

Will my child get confused learning two languages at once?
No. Children naturally pick up several languages at the same time, and learning two together can boost problem-solving and flexible thinking. The program is designed to keep it clear and fun.
What if my child has never studied Arabic or Spanish?
That’s fine. Instructors check each child’s level and adjust activities and vocabulary, from complete beginners to children with some background.
How are Arabic and Spanish taught?
Through a play-based curriculum that mixes language with social and emotional skills. For example, during food week campers role-play ordering a meal and solving problems politely when an order goes wrong.

Activities

What will my child learn in STEM?
Math puzzles, science experiments, coding and engineering challenges, all hands-on.
What is Islamic Discovery?
An optional block, twice a week, where children learn about Islam through stories, art and activities that build character, including Quran memorization.
What kinds of creative arts are there?
Painting, crafts, storytelling, creative writing and simple media projects.
What makes this camp different?
It combines language immersion, STEAM, creative learning, leadership and outdoor play, guided by our own curriculum with clear learning goals for each week.

Getting there

Do you provide transportation?
Yes, for $100 a week. Transport for Friday field trips is included for everyone.
Can my child attend only some weeks?
Yes. Register for any week or weeks that suit your family.
