Student Life

A Student Life Built to Explore, Compete, Serve and Belong

Life at Falcon reaches well beyond the classroom: field trips, clubs, competitions, sports and service, all grounded in faith and good character.

Explore Clubs → Book a Tour

A Values-Based Community Where Every Child Grows

Falcon is a values-based school where faith and character shape everyday life. Students serve their community, build and test ideas through STEAM, and follow their interests in clubs and enrichment, so every child grows in mind, body and heart.

Faith & Character

Daily prayer together, and a school culture that values honesty, kindness and service.

Community Engagement

Service projects, family events and townhalls that bring students, teachers and families together.

STEM & STEAM

Robotics labs, coding and hands-on STEAM projects where students use what they learn.

Whole-Child Growth

Arts, sports and languages after school, with counseling support when extra help is needed.

Trips & Field Trips

Learning travels, from a farm field trip in science to our fall visit to Cox Farms. Every week of Summer Camp ends with a Friday field trip or celebration.

See Trip Photos →

Competitions

Students prepare all year for the Annual Qur’an Competition, the Arabic Poetry Contest and the Arabic Spelling Bee, while clubs train for VEX Robotics, MathCON, Future City and the KidWind Challenge.

See Events Through the Year →

Sports & Movement

Basketball, running and martial arts clubs keep students moving, and PE builds toward team sports in the Middle Years. Chantilly has a soccer field and basketball, volleyball and badminton courts.

See Sports Clubs →

STEM & Robotics

Students build and code robots in Design class at Ashburn. After school, clubs add VEX Robotics, Solar & Wind Energy, NASA / Aerospace and Science Fair.

Explore STEM Clubs →

Service & Leadership

Middle Years students plan and lead Service as Action projects, like a neighborhood litter clean-up. In high school, leadership grows through community service and mentorship.
