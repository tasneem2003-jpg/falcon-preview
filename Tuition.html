Need help with tuition?

The Falcon Family Support Program — need-based, confidential, decided by a third party.

How it works

Support is based on your family's financial need, not your child's grades. We use SSS (School and Student Services) so your finances are reviewed independently and privately.

  • Applying never affects admission. The admissions team doesn't see your aid file.
  • Covers base tuition. Books, transport and optional services are not covered.
  • Can be combined with the sibling discount and referral credits.
  • Renew yearly. Decisions usually arrive within 3–4 weeks of a complete file.

3 steps · about 30 minutes

  1. 1

    Open the SSS Family Portal

    Create an account and start the Parents' Financial Statement.

  2. 2

    Enter Falcon's school code

    So your report reaches us: 201332

  3. 3

    Upload your tax documents

    Most recent W-2s and 1040. Photos from your phone are fine.

Deadline: July 1. Late requests are considered only if funds remain.
