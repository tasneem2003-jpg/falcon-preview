Admissions 2026–27

How to Apply

Falcon Academy is now accepting applications for the 2026–2027 school year, from Kindergarten to Grade 11.

We’re thrilled you’re exploring Falcon for your child. The application is simple, and our admissions team is here to answer your questions at every step.

Start Application → Email Admissions

Application fee

$150 per child

  • Paid with the application form
  • Your application is complete once it is paid
  • Non-refundable

Three Steps to Apply

One application covers every child you are enrolling.

  1. 1

    Step 1: Start Application

    Fill out the application once for all the children you are enrolling.

    Start Application →
  2. 2

    Step 2: Pay

    Pay the non-refundable $150 application fee for each child by credit card, through the secure payment page.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Complete Application

    Submit the items listed in your assigned Tasks, and upload all the required documents online.

    In your parent portal

What Happens Next

The admissions office reviews applications as space allows. Once every checklist item is in, including standardized test results and any placement test or interview for new students, you receive the decision letter and contract electronically.

Questions? admissions@falconacademy.org
