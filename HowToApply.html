How to apply

One online application for all your children. Already a Falcon family? Re-enroll in the Parent Portal instead.

Now open for 2026–27

Kindergarten to Grade 12

$150 per child

Non-refundable · paid inside the form

Decision by email

Rolling, as space is available

Have these ready before you start

The list of documents for each child appears in your portal after you apply.

  • Each child’s details name and grade
  • Your email address for your portal login
  • Credit card $150 per child, non-refundable

The four steps

You do steps 1 and 2. The admissions office handles the rest and contacts you by email.

  1. 1

    Step 1: Apply and pay online

    Fill one form for all the children you are enrolling. At the end of the same form, pay the non-refundable $150 fee for each child by credit card. Your application is complete once the fee is paid.

    One form per familyReferral or Legacy code goes in “Referred By”

    Inside the form

    Instructions → Parent/Guardian → Student(s) → Referral → Summary, then payment.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Set up your portal and upload documents

    After you pay, you are taken to the parent portal. Check your inbox for an email to set your password, then upload the documents on each child’s checklist.

    No email?

    Check your spam folder, or contact admissions@falconacademy.org.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Review and placement

    Once your file is complete, the admissions office reviews it based on space availability. This may include standardized test results, and a placement test or interview when needed.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Decision & enroll

    The decision letter and enrollment contract arrive by email. Sign the contract and choose a payment plan.

    Pick 10-month · 2 payments · pay in full (−5%)

    Need aid?

    Apply for the Falcon Family Support Program on SSS by July 1, after you receive an offer. It never affects the admission decision.

Does one of these apply to you?

Each one tells you exactly what to do — nothing extra to fill out.

I’m already a Falcon family

Don’t fill this form. Re-enroll your children from your existing account in the Parent Portal.

Go to the Parent Portal →

I'm applying for 2+ children

Add every child to the same application in step 1. Each additional child gets a $450 tuition discount.

See step 1 →

I have a referral code

In step 1, enter the referring family in the “Referred By” field. If your child is accepted, both families get a $500 tuition credit. It can't be added after you submit.

No code? See referral program →

A founding family gave me a Legacy Voucher

Enter the voucher code in your application in step 1. If your child is accepted, you get a $1,000 tuition discount. Valid Feb 1 – Aug 1, 2026.
